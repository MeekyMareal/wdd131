Site Purpose

I believe that it is important for any professional to have a solid online presence that will present their skills and competencies in the best possible way.

Such a website is helpful because it allows me to demonstrate my competencies and skills to my future employers show them my practical coding skills and experience in developing real projects

Color Schema

  • Primary Color (#003366): Header, navigation, and primary buttons.
  • Accent Color (#FFFFFF): Button text, icons, and highlights on dark backgrounds.
  • Neutral Background (#F4F4F4): Main page background and card containers.
  • Dark Text (#212529): Body text and paragraph content.
  • Primary / Dark Color (#000000): Headings, text, and main navigation on light backgrounds.
  • Muted Gray Text (#666666): Subheadings and secondary descriptions.
