Benin City, Nigeria

Nigeria is a culturally diverse westernized country located in North Africa. Due to its historical status in the region, many local ethnicities, languages, rituals, and customs have developed and are still popular among locals and visitors to this day. Its culture and legacy are deeply connected to its historical landmarks, traditional cuisine, music, and art.

Thus, the cultural significance of Benin City and Nigeria is deeply embedded into the local population and their way of life. The region possesses a great variety of ancient and modern artifacts, which display the diversity and beauty of the local traditions and customs.
